Common questions

How do I replay the tutorial? Tap How to play on the main menu.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Summit Stack LLC (“we”) makes Swayambhu Kora (“the game”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store or send any personal information to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your best scores, play history, highest level reached, tutorial progress, avatar choices and settings (such as sound and on-screen buttons) are saved only on your device so the game can remember them. We cannot see them. Deleting the game removes them.

Network use

The iPhone, iPad and Android apps work completely offline and make no network connections.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
